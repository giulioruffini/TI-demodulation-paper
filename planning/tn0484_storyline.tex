\smallskip
The field term survives the slow-synapse reduction, and recurrent feedback
changes the steady response through the slope of the local transfer curve.
A short addition in Theory makes this connection explicit, distinguishes a
cellular voltage response from the model's current input, and separates the
bare transfer curve from a curve fitted after internal recurrence has settled.
The full dimensional derivation and a proposed Zhao-model calibration study
are in the companion note for Raul. The reduction and feedback gain are prior
results, attributed to Ruffini and to Clusella and colleagues.

All earlier article text, results, figures, equations, and the shared abstract
remain. The editing baseline is \texttt{9915f13}; draft20 remains the recorded
reading baseline. Ricardo's FEM additions remain as integrated in draft28,
with their single-subject scope and unresolved convergence qualifications.

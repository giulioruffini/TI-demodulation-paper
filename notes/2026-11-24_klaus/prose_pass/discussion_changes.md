## Changes by paragraph

- "Cellular mixing \cite... and network amplification": the paragraph now opens with prior support and the unmeasured combined transfer, which states the point of the heading, and then gives our JR/QIF result. Prior work and our models are separated, and "short-time cases ... are access assumptions" is kept.

- "Missey et al. report": the carrier pairs move into a parenthesis attributed to their Methods. The colon list of reasons becomes "challenges that access hypothesis without testing it directly, because ...", so each confound now reads as a reason the test is indirect.

The two pattern claims (fixed quadratic pattern is algebraic, centroid motion is subject- and montage-specific) are contrasted with "whereas". - "These maps generate a hypothesis about Violante et al.'s findings": "The connection to ... is a hypothesis generated by" becomes active voice. "relative to sham ... and to 1:1" removes an ambiguity. All P values and voxel percentages are unchanged.
- "In IXI002": "whereas" now links the 24% steady-source rise to the 0.56 rectified-beat scaling, which makes the two orderings explicit, and one repeated "fall" is dropped. The slow-readout sentence now joins its two halves with "but retains" and "and".

- "At stronger fields": the undefined shorthand "crossing a circuit Hopf" becomes "crossing the circuit's Hopf bifurcation", and "low carriers" becomes "low carrier frequencies".
- "The common TI/tACS amplifier": the long paragraph is split. This first part keeps the amplifier claim, its consistency citations and the state-shift caveat.

The signpost "The amplifier's temporal selectivity is a separate question" is deleted as narration. "order-ten quality" becomes Q∼10, and "making detuning consequential" becomes "so detuning matters". The single-column caveat moves to the end of the paragraph, beside the near-Hopf slowing it qualifies.

\subsection{Static rate with dynamic synapses}
In NMM2, $u$ denotes the postsynaptic potential divided by the pathway's
steady voltage gain, so it has the same units as the presynaptic firing
rate $r$ \cite{clusella2023}. For a self-coupled population,
\begin{equation}
\tau_s^2\ddot u+2\tau_s\dot u+u=r,\qquad
r\simeq \frac{F(Q)}{\tau_m}.
\label{eq:slow_synapse}
\end{equation}
The filter equation is exact for this synapse. The rate follows the static
curve when $Q$ varies slowly relative to population relaxation. For the single excitatory synapse
of \S\ref{sec:nmm}, $a=1/\tau_s$, the gain is $A\tau_s$, and the physical potential is
$A\tau_s u$ in mV.

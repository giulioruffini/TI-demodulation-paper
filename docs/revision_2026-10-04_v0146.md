# v0.14.6: review of Kaiti's v0.14.5 with four corrections

Giulio asked whether Kaiti's v0.14.5 (commit `394b68c`, October 3) needed revision. This
pass reviews it against v0.14.4 (`2ccea30`) and applies a small set of corrections. The
weak-field effective-response framing that K authorized is kept as Kaiti wrote it; the
section order, figure set, equations, simulations, and parameters are unchanged. Release,
GitHub push, Overleaf synchronization, Calliope cut, and circulation remain held.

## What v0.14.5 does well

The effective second-order coefficient is defined correctly: with the cosine-amplitude
convention the memoryless limit is $\Sigma''(v^*)/2$, matching Eq. 10. The derivation of
$F(Q)$ as the Lorentzian average of the single-neuron rate $f(q)=\sqrt{q}/\pi$ is right and
names the two single-cell features in its curvature (threshold recruitment, concave rate
increase). The R10 sentence is now consistent with its formula: $\eta_{\rm det}\propto
\varepsilon$, so the relative efficiency falls as the input weakens. The Plovie 2025
sentence was checked against the published abstract: Hodgkin--Huxley and
Frankenhaeuser--Huxley replicate the observed responses; leaky, exponential, and
adaptive-exponential integrate-and-fire models do not; the quadratic model was not
tested. The Cifra bibliography entry matches the article (Chem. Soc. Rev. 55, 7568--7616,
2026, doi:10.1039/D3CS00244F). Both builds and the prose checker were rerun here and are
clean (47/31/18 pages).

## Corrections applied

**Abstracts.** v0.14.5 dropped the consequence of the shared amplifier, "so resonance
alone gives TI no efficiency advantage", from both abstracts while the Conclusion kept
it. Restored in both (TN abstract 236 words; JNE structured abstract under 300). The TN
abstract's "membrane attenuation counted once" is again said in words, "entering once,
through access", as the JNE abstract already does.

**Gate filter (§2.6).** v0.14.5 said an additional gate filter "would duplicate any
dynamics already included" in the detector, which is only half of K's position of
October 2: a gate filter must not duplicate dynamics the detector contains, and where
the detector lacks them (QIF has no sodium-channel kinetics) it needs its own model or
measurement. Both halves are now stated.

**Cifra citation (Discussion).** The clause was attached to the voltage-gated-channel
sentence by a semicolon without saying what it was for. It now states the point: at
stimulation fields of order 1 V/m the channels respond through membrane polarization,
not through direct action of the field on the protein; the structural effects Cifra et
al. review require 10 kV/m to GV/m, and the review places fields below 0.1 kV/m outside
its scope, where it expects direct effects to be negligible against thermal noise. The
field range and the thermal-noise expectation are the review's own statements (§4.1).

**Discussion, first paragraph.** v0.14.5 removed the sentence that cellular mixing and
circuit amplification are not competing explanations. Restored, since removing an
explanation needs K's agreement and the point carries the paragraph's conclusion.

**Notation.** Table 4 gains a row for $\chi^{(2)}(\omega_2,-\omega_1)$, the symbol
v0.14.5 introduced in §2.2.

**Planning files.** The abstract file is regenerated from the manuscript so the two stay
identical, and its template header (date, status, comparison, word count) is restored.
The storyline driver is relabeled v0.14.6; its map and the three open placement
decisions are Kaiti's and are unchanged.

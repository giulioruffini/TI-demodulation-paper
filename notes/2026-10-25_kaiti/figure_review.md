# Draft24 integrated figure review

Kaiti, October 25, 2026. All 26 external figure assets were rendered and viewed
individually, together with the compiled circuit diagram (27 figures total).
Thirteen plotted figures changed, and the LaNMM setup received TeX panel letters.
The file list and PDF text measurements are in `figure_textcheck.txt` beside this note.

The repairs clear the originally reported legend and colorbar collisions. The
QIF phase folds now occupy separate axes, with the same cached spikes, smoothing,
averaging, normalization and spectra; an AST comparison verifies those numerical
functions and the phase-fold loop are unchanged. In the timing figure, short
legends leave the spectral peaks and bars visible. The feasibility panel (c)
has additional vertical headroom for its legend. Shorter laminar colorbar labels
and fixed layout after text sizing avoid cropped tick and axis labels.

Base type in the repaired PDF figures is at least approximately 6 points at the
placed width; the QIF figures print at 6.4 points or larger. The unchanged PING
map is 5.96 points (rounded to 6.0). Small mathtext fractions and subscripts are
not base-font failures. The reported SNIC/HB collision is a bounding-box overlap;
the letters are separate in the rendered image. The MPR 10^3/300 finding is an
off-view tick. No clipped visible text remains in the inspected figures.

The revised raster captions identify the separate phase folds; the laminar
captions define the power differences and arbitrary units retained in the
short labels. Panel letters, frequency markers, contours, normalizations, and
the single-carrier control match their captions. The twelve-second settling
correction and its limitations are recorded in the draft24 revision record.

Final article/supplement page inspection includes the circuit, the complete
raster figures with captions, both laminar maps, and the schematic overlay.
The main labels and historical supplementary numbering are retained.

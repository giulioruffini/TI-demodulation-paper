\pagetitle{Scope, length, and verification}{Approved work proceeds before the next author reading}
The main article retains the full PING system, core MPR equations and static-rate
curve, Table 2 access evidence, amplitude-matching conventions, and the compact
crossover derivation. The feasibility figure replaces scattered access-budget
explanations. The theta-neuron pilot passed step and window checks but changed by 12\%
under population doubling, exceeding its 5\% criterion. Its scope is therefore
qualitative; the equal-RMS gamma control remains a mean-field result.

The revised abstract distinguishes static and dynamic detection, names the
single-head illustration, and describes JR population-rate modulation. The
steady effect at depth, 2--5\% matched-polarization comparison, shared tACS gain,
and scoped boundary restriction remain. The multicompartment statement preserves
bilinear current scaling without assuming a universal common projection vector.

The article falls from 14,522 to about 13,000 words including captions and
article appendices, with the new results included. Repeated explanations are
consolidated and the full prediction/evidence matrix is supplementary. The
article remains above the journal's 12,000-word norm.
No integral model is relocated merely to improve that count. The LaNMM study
remains supplementary. The efficiency-bound placement remains unchanged.

A NumPy-only FEM consumer and checked exporter are prepared for derived fields,
coordinates, tissue and ROI labels, orientations, units, and hashes. The IXI002
per-node inputs are unavailable locally; export and real-data comparison remain
pending before release.
Validation covers analytic arithmetic, numerical convergence, retained claims,
article/supplement builds, references, rendered pages, and a redline against
both the editing and reading baselines. Klaus checks the integrated revision.

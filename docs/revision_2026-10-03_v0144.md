## What changed in the manuscript

**Abstract.** The TN abstract states the result in place of "We show how"; the
time-scale sentences now carry the coexistence claim they justify; "membrane filtering
counted once" is said in words; the dose-map sentence states its reason; the open
questions close the paragraph (238 words). The JNE structured abstract keeps its form
and changes the same phrase (251 words by a plain count, under the 300-word cap).

**One factual slip corrected.** The opening paragraph of Results §4.1 described the
input as having "lines only at $f_c$ and $f_c\pm\Delta f$", which is the three-line AM
surrogate. Figure 3 is driven by two tones, as its caption and Methods state, so the
text now reads "lines only at the two tones $f_c\mp\Delta f/2$" and quotes the 11.1 Hz
beat the caption reports.

**Notation collisions removed (todo items).** The leading Jacobian eigenvalue pair is
now $\mu_\pm=-\gamma\pm i\omega_0$ in §2.5, §3.1, and Table 4, leaving $\lambda$ to the
field coupling. The NMM1 population indices in Eq. 3 are $X\leftarrow Y$, matching the
LaNMM usage $X\in\{P_1,P_2\}$ and freeing $m$ for the modulation depth. Eq. 8 now
writes both sides of the access relation as vectors.

**Acronyms expanded at first use.** PING (§2.3), RK4 (§3.1), FFT (§3.2), PSP
(Appendix A head note), PWM-TI (Discussion).

**Grammar and register.** "the PING as run is therefore computes" fixed. Removed the
emphasis words *genuine* (outside the two-tone-waveform sense), *clean*, *textbook*,
*precisely*, *exactly* where it carried no mathematics, and *Concretely*; "The core
result is Fig. 3" replaced by the result; "Sharp test" column renamed "Proposed test";
"before the population nonlinearity ever saw it" and "Finite is not zero" rewritten;
the defensive clause in Appendix D ("we do not offer it as a measured single-cell
property") stated as a fact; the cadence opener "Two features of that limit decide the
reading" deleted; "As a third, independent test" corrected to "As an independent test"
(the Results opener counts two rungs). The revision note "The earlier version of these
maps showed only the positive part of the change" was removed from Appendix E, as it
describes a draft rather than the science.

**Paragraphs.** Single paragraphs of 300–400 words in Methods §3.1–3.4, Results §4.6
and §4.8, Theory §2.7, and Appendices D and E were split at their natural seams. No
sentence was removed in the splits. The Figure 4 caption no longer repeats the body's
Arnold-tongue sentence and points to Figure S4 in one clause.

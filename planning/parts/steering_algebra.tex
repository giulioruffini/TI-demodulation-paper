\subsection{Envelope and mean-square changes under current-ratio steering}\label{supp:steering-algebra}
The mean-square polarization
changes by $\mathcal I_{1:3}/\mathcal I_{1:1}
=(\mathcal P_1^2+9\mathcal P_2^2)/[4(\mathcal P_1^2+\mathcal P_2^2)]$,
exceeding one only where $|\mathcal P_2|>\sqrt{3/5}|\mathcal P_1|
\approx0.775|\mathcal P_1|$. Its hippocampal mean rises by 24\% and its focus
moves 4.5~mm posteriorly. Equal summed currents therefore do not match local
RMS polarization.

Local envelope excursion increases under 1:3 where
$|\mathcal P_1|>2|\mathcal P_2|$, reaching $1.5\times$ where
$|\mathcal P_1|\geq3|\mathcal P_2|$. A selective or thresholded envelope
response therefore remains possible despite the lower ROI mean excursion.


The mean-square increase holds across the eight montage--label--tissue
variants in Table~\ref{tab:fem-mean-variants}: $17.2$--$29.7\%$, while the
ROI-mean envelope excursion falls by $43.9$--$46.2\%$ and both beat maps fall by $25\%$.
Focus fraction does not enter an ROI mean; these eight variants are the
same two montages, two labels, and two tissue selections used in the
32-combination focus check.

The local inequalities hold node by node. Along $\mathbf n^*$ in the
baseline Violante/FreeSurfer GM+WM ROI, mean square rises at 1,353 of 1,568
nodes ($86.3\%$), whereas the envelope rises at two ($0.13\%$). Across the
eight variants the fractions are $80.8$--$90.9\%$ and $0$--$0.2\%$,
respectively. Thus a selectively weighted envelope response remains possible,
but its increasing subset is small in this example. The envelope rises at
$8.9\%$ of reference nodes for Violante and $14.0\%$ for Yatsuda; this
reference includes all gray matter outside the ROI, not only cortex.
These are equally weighted node counts along the fixed $\mathbf n^*$,
not radial projections or volume-weighted fractions.

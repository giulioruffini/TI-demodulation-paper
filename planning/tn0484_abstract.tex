\newcommand{\absObjective}{To determine how neural populations transduce temporal-interference (TI)
stimulation through three stages in series: membrane access, nonlinear detection of
envelope features, and circuit amplification.}

\newcommand{\absApproach}{Our mesoscale model assigns access an effective membrane time and analyzes detection and amplification in a Jansen--Rit column, an exact quadratic
integrate-and-fire (QIF) mean field, and its spiking network. Maps use one head model; input budgets assume field
coupling and membrane times.}

\newcommand{\absResults}{For weak fields, static quadratic detection generates a beat proportional to the
carrier-polarization product and a signed, direct-current-like (DC-like) steady source
proportional to their summed squares. At the pinned
Jansen--Rit state, 0.1--0.3 mV per carrier gives TI sources about
2--5\% of leading-order linear sources of transcranial alternating-current
stimulation (tACS) or direct-current stimulation (tDCS) at equal per-carrier and direct-input
polarizations. At fixed synaptic input, QIF's steady rate shift crosses from curvature- to
positive-slope-controlled rectification as carrier frequency rises. Changing current ratio moves hippocampal envelope and mean-square centroids
oppositely but only rescales the fixed-state beat map. Near a Hopf bifurcation, resonant gain grows inversely with modal damping
and is shared with tACS. In the pinned Jansen--Rit column, coupling changes gain
independently of detection, chiefly amplifying beat-locked population-rate
modulation with little mean-rate change. At stronger inputs, the steady source alone drives the QIF mean field across its gamma
threshold, as does an unmodulated carrier matched in root-mean-square input.}

\newcommand{\absSignificance}{Weak-field beat dose follows the polarization product rather than the envelope minimum.
Resonance amplifies TI and matched tACS equally, so it confers no efficiency advantage.
The steady source can act at depth; its sign depends on detector state and dynamics,
not carrier polarity. For uniform scalar coupling and detection in homogeneous
conductivity, its magnitude has no strict interior maximum. Polarization measurements and amplitude and frequency sweeps test the
stages; physiological kHz coupling remains uncalibrated.}
